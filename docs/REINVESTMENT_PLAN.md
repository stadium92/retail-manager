# 📊 How to Spend the 50% Reinvestment Budget

**Rule**: Don't spend the 50% all at once. Spend it in priority order as revenue comes in.

> **If you have < 20 customers → spend on SALES.**
> **If you have > 20 customers → spend on AUTOMATION/TOOLS.**

---

## Month 1 (NOW) — Get Your First 10 Sales

| Priority | Item | Cost | Why |
|----------|------|------|-----|
| 1 | **Print 200 flyers** | 5,000 FCFA | Physical presence in Niamana shops |
| 2 | **Transport budget** | 15,000 FCFA/week | Moto-taxi to visit 5 shops/day |
| 3 | **External SSD** | 40,000 FCFA | Backups of customer data (trust builder) |

## Month 2 — Scale to 30 Sales

| Priority | Item | Cost | Why |
|----------|------|------|-----|
| 4 | **Reseller commissions** | Variable | Pay 15k per sale they close for you |
| 5 | **Business cards** | 10,000 FCFA | Leave with every prospect |
| 6 | **Phone credit** | 10,000 FCFA/month | WhatsApp calls for support |

## Month 3 — Automate Development

| Priority | Item | Cost | Why |
|----------|------|------|-----|
| 7 | **Claude API / Copilot** | 50,000 FCFA/month | Ship features 5x faster |
| 8 | **Domain + hosting** | 15,000 FCFA/year | Professional landing page for credibility |

## Month 6+ — Hire Help

| Priority | Item | Cost | Why |
|----------|------|------|-----|
| 9 | **Part-time support** | 100,000 FCFA/month | Handle WhatsApp + installs so you code |
| 10 | **Better laptop** | 200,000-400,000 FCFA | Only if current one slows you down |

---

## The Key Insight

The first 50,000 FCFA of reinvestment should go 100% toward **getting more sales** (flyers + transport), not tools. Tools come after you have consistent income.
