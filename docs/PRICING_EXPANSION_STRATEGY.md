## What to Tell Customers NOW About the Future

**When a shop owner asks "Is there an online version?":**

> **"Oui, la version en ligne arrive dans quelques mois. Quand elle sort, ça sera 100,000 FCFA par an. Mais vous, en tant que partenaire fondateur, vous aurez accès à TOUT gratuitement, à vie. C'est pour ça que le prix de 50,000 est une opportunité — c'est maintenant ou jamais."**

> *(Yes, the online version is coming in a few months. When it launches, it will be 100k/year. But you, as a founding partner, will get access to EVERYTHING for free, for life. That's why the 50k price is an opportunity — it's now or never.)*

This does 3 things:
1. Shows them you have a **roadmap** (you're serious, not a hobby project)
2. Makes the 50k feel like a **steal** compared to 100k/year
3. Creates **urgency** (this price disappears)

---

## The "Founding Partner" Promise (What They Actually Get)

**What's INCLUDED forever at 50k**:
- All offline features (current + future)
- All software updates (V1.0, V2.0, etc.)
- Cloud sync when it launches (no extra fee)
- Remote access from their phone

**What's NOT included** (they pay extra later):
- Additional devices beyond 2 (10,000 FCFA/device/year)
- Multi-store management (separate license per store)
- Custom reports or integrations (quoted per request)

---

## How to Raise Prices Without Losing Trust

**Rule**: NEVER raise prices on existing customers. Only on new ones.

| When | Action |
|------|--------|
| After 50 sales | Announce on WhatsApp Status: *"Nos 50 places de partenaire fondateur sont complètes ! Nouvelle offre : 75,000 FCFA"* |
| After 150 sales | Switch to annual subscription: *"Retail Manager V1.0 est maintenant en ligne ! 100,000 FCFA/an"* |
| After 500 sales | Introduce tiers: Standard (100k) vs Premium (200k) |

**The psychology**: Each price increase validates the early buyers' decision. They feel smart for buying early. They tell their friends: *"I got it for 50k, now it's 100k!"* — which is free advertising.

## The Online Revenue Model (Month 6+)

Once you add cloud features, you unlock **recurring revenue**:

| Revenue Stream | Price | Frequency |
|---------------|-------|-----------|
| New licenses | 100,000 FCFA | Annual subscription |
| Cloud sync add-on (for founding partners who want multi-device beyond 2) | 25,000 FCFA | Annual |
| Installation service | 15,000 FCFA | One-time |
| Training (group of 5 shops) | 50,000 FCFA | One-time |
| Custom reports | 30,000-100,000 FCFA | Per request |

**Monthly projection at 200 subscribers**:
- 200 × 100k/year = 20M FCFA/year = **~1,700,000 FCFA/month**
- Minus hosting/server: ~50,000/month
- Minus support staff: ~200,000/month
- **Net**: ~1,450,000 FCFA/month
