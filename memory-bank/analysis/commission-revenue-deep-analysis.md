## 1. Where You're LOSING Money (Commission Cluster)

### Single Sale Math (50k with Royalty Reseller at OLD 30%)

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Sale Price | 50,000 FCFA |
| Reseller Commission (30%) | -15,000 FCFA |
| **Net to Business** | **35,000 FCFA** |
| → Reinvest (50%) | 17,500 FCFA |
| → Your Salary (30%) | 10,500 FCFA |
| → Emergency (20%) | 7,000 FCFA |

Your salary per sale at 30%: **10,500 FCFA** — too tight.

### Money Leaks Identified

| Leak | Cost | Solution |
|------|------|----------|
| 30% commission on 50k product | 15,000/sale | Drop to 15% for royalty holders |
| Lifetime updates for free | Unknown future cost | KEEP (costs you nothing to build) |
| Lifetime support | Hours/week forever | Limit to 1 year free, then 25k/year |
| Installation + Training time | 1-3 hours/client | Charge 15,000 FCFA for setup |
| Hardware incompatibility | Lost sales | Create minimum specs checklist |

## 2. Commission Split Rules

### When You Sell TOGETHER With Reseller

| Scenario | Reseller Gets | Why |
|----------|--------------|-----|
| Reseller sells alone | 30% (15,000) | They did all the work |
| You sell together (like today) | 15% (7,500) | You both contributed |
| Reseller brings lead, you close | 20% (10,000) | They opened the door |
| You sell alone, reseller introduced once | 10% (5,000) | Just a referral |

### Reseller Types & Compensation

| Reseller Type | Commission | Equity | Total |
|---------------|-----------|--------|-------|
| **Royalty Reseller** (has equity) | **15%** | 2% of profits | Commission + long-term |
| **Commission-Only Reseller** | **30%** | 0% | Cash only |
| **Referral Partner** (sends leads) | **10%** | 0% | Minimal effort |

## 4. Pyramid Commissions — REJECTED

**Hard no.** Reasons: Illegal in UEMOA, ruins reputation, math doesn't work (45% gone in 3 levels).

**Alternative**: Simple 2-level referral:
- Level 1 (Direct Reseller): 15-30%
- Level 2 (Reseller's referral): 5,000 FCFA flat bonus
- NO Level 3. Ever.
