## 1. Data Entry vs. Training — Who Does What?

| Task | Who | Why |
|------|-----|-----|
| **Training** | **YOU (Founder)** | You know the app best. Training builds trust. Client bonds with YOU. |
| **Data Entry** | **RESELLER** | Repetitive work. Doesn't need deep knowledge. Reseller earns extra. |
| **Installation** | **EITHER** | Teach reseller to install. Saves 1-2 hours/client. |

### Workflow Per New Client:
1. Reseller closes the sale → collects 50k
2. Reseller does data entry (1-3 hours) → charges client 15k, keeps it as bonus
3. **YOU** come for 30-min training → builds authority
4. Client sees TWO people supporting them → feels premium

| **Samedi** | 🏪 Field Sales (5 shops) | 📊 Review numbers/plan | 🛋️ REST |
| **Dimanche** | **OFF** | **OFF** | **OFF** |

### The Math:
- **Sales days**: 4 days × 5 shops = 20 visits/week → ~4 sales
- **Coding days**: 2 full days + 3 evenings = ~20 hours/week
- **Rest**: 4 evenings + all Sunday = sustainable
- **Social**: Friday evening + Sunday

### Non-Negotiable Rules:
1. **Sunday is OFF.** No code, no sales, no WhatsApp.
2. **No coding past 21h.** Sleep > features.
3. **Eat properly.** Budget 5,000 FCFA/day minimum for food.
4. **WhatsApp support hours: 9h-17h only.** Tell clients upfront.

## 3. Exit Strategy — Selling to Orange/Malitel

### Why They Would Buy

| Buyer | Why They'd Want It |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Orange Mali** | Digital backbone for small business. Your app + Orange Money = perfect combo. |
| **Malitel** | Trailing Orange, need to differentiate. |
| **A bank (BDM, Ecobank)** | POS integration + credit scoring from sales data. |
| **International (Jumia, Wave)** | Expanding into retail management in West Africa. |

### Estimated Acquisition Price

| Metric | At 50 clients | At 200 clients | At 500 clients |
|--------|--------------|----------------|----------------|
| Annual Recurring Revenue | ~0 (one-time) | ~20M FCFA | ~50M FCFA |
| Typical Multiple | 3-5x | 3-5x | 5-10x |
| **Estimated Sale Price** | **5-15M FCFA** (too low) | **60-100M FCFA** | **250-500M FCFA** |

**Rule: Don't sell before 200+ active clients.** Below that, no leverage.

### The Payment Gateway Play (Highest Value Feature)

1. **Before payment gateway**: Stock management tool (commodity)
2. **After payment gateway**: Financial platform (10x valuation)

Orange doesn't just buy your app — they buy your **distribution network** (200+ shops) and the ability to process payments through THEM.

### How to Approach Them

**Don't pitch "buy my app."** Pitch this:

> "I have 200 shops in Bamako using my software daily. I want to integrate Orange Money as the default payment method. Can we discuss a partnership?"

This makes Orange come to YOU. They'll realize:
- 200 shops × 50 transactions/day = 10,000 daily transactions
- Orange Money takes 1-2% per transaction
- That's recurring revenue for THEM

**They'll either**: Partner (you keep the app, they get payment flow) or Acquire (they buy you out).

### Client Transition Options If You Sell

| Option | What Happens to Clients | Your Obligation |
|--------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Asset Sale** | Buyer takes over everything. You're gone. | 3-month transition support in the deal. |
| **License Deal** | You keep the code. They license it. | You maintain it for a fee. |
| **Acqui-hire** | They hire you + buy the app. | You work for them 2 years. Salary + bonus. |

**Best option**: **Acqui-hire.** Lump sum (50-100M FCFA) + salary (500k-1M/month for 2 years). Clients get continued support. Everyone wins.

### What NOT to Do

**Don't sell and abandon your 50 founding partners.**
1. Ensure buyer commits to honoring lifetime updates
2. Include 3-month transition where you introduce new support team
3. Send each client a personal message explaining the change

### Exit Timeline

| Phase | When | Focus |
|-------|------|-------|
| **Build** | Months 1-6 | Get to 200 clients. Add payment gateway. |
| **Prove** | Months 7-12 | Show consistent revenue. Get to 500 clients. |
| **Pitch** | Month 12-18 | Approach Orange/Malitel with data. |
| **Exit** | Month 18-24 | Negotiate acquisition or partnership. |

**Estimated exit price at 500 clients with payment gateway: 250-500M FCFA ($400k-$800k USD)**

Your 50 clients aren't just customers. They're your **distribution network, your data, and your bargaining chip.** Every shop using your app is a node in a network that Orange/Malitel would pay millions to access.

> **Don't sell a seed. Sell a tree.**
