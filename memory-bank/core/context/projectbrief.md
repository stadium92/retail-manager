# Project Brief - Retail Manager

## Core Goal
A comprehensive digital logistics and retail management platform designed to digitize and optimize small retail operations through AI-powered insights, sales analytics, and multi-store management. **Crucially, it serves as an Accountability & Anti-Theft System to prevent the severe revenue loss (up to 20%) common in emerging markets.**

## Key Objectives
- **Strict Accountability & Anti-Theft**: Implement blind closeouts, strict role-based permissions (cashiers cannot delete sales or edit stock), and immutable audit logs to stop active theft.
- **Offline-First Hybrid Architecture**: Ensure the POS works flawlessly without internet (validating the reality of no Wi-Fi modems in target stores), syncing with the cloud (Supabase) when available for multi-store "hub and spoke" management.
- **Low-End Hardware Compatibility**: Optimize for Intel Dual Core CPUs and 2.5GB RAM.
- **Four-Sided System**: Distinct interfaces for Master (Owner), Worker, Deliverer, and Customer.
- **AI Intelligence**: Provide a "ChatGPT for retail managers" experience with edge AI analyzing sales patterns and inventory.
- **One-Time Payment Model**: Go-to-market strategy focuses on a perpetual license for the offline app, with optional low-cost yearly add-ons for cloud sync, matching local purchasing preferences.

## Target Audience
Small retail store owners, pharmacies, and independent retailers managing one or multiple locations, specifically in emerging markets (like Mali) facing high theft rates, low connectivity, and subscription fatigue.

## Success Criteria
- Drastic reduction in inventory shrinkage and employee theft.
- Successful operation on target low-end hardware without internet.
- Complete data synchronization between local and cloud planes for remote monitoring.
- AI activation after one month of sales data collection.
- High conversion rate via the one-time payment pricing model.
