# Project Brief - Retail Manager

## Core Goal
A comprehensive digital logistics and retail management platform designed to digitize and optimize small retail operations through AI-powered insights, sales analytics, and multi-store management.

## Key Objectives
- **Offline-First Hybrid Architecture**: Ensure the POS works flawlessly without internet, syncing with the cloud (Supabase) when available.
- **Low-End Hardware Compatibility**: Optimize for Intel Dual Core CPUs and 2.5GB RAM.
- **Four-Sided System**: Distinct interfaces for Master (Owner), Worker, Deliverer, and Customer.
- **AI Intelligence**: Provide a "ChatGPT for retail managers" experience with edge AI analyzing sales patterns and inventory.

## Target Audience
Small retail store owners, pharmacies, and independent retailers managing one or multiple locations.

## Success Criteria
- Successful operation on target low-end hardware.
- Complete data synchronization between local and cloud planes.
- AI activation after one month of sales data collection.
- Reliable remote monitoring for store owners.
